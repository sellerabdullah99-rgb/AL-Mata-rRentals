Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Service

AL-Matar Rentals started in 2018 with just one car and a simple belief: Karachi deserves a car rental service that's honest, reliable, and treats every customer like family.

What began as a small operation in Gulshan Iqbal has grown into a fleet of 50+ verified vehicles serving thousands of families, corporates, and travelers across all of Karachi — and beyond.

Today, AL-Matar Rentals is the #1 choice in Gulshan Iqbal with a 4.9-star rating across 1,500+ real reviews. Every car we deliver carries our promise: clean, safe, on time, no hidden charges.
